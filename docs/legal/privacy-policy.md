# TaxSight Privacy Policy

> **Draft v1.0** — Prepared 2026-05-10
> **Review required by legal counsel before deployment**

---

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how TaxSight LLC ("TaxSight," "we," "us," "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our Service.

This policy applies to all users of TaxSight, including individual taxpayers and tax professionals using the Pro+ tier.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Information**: name, email address, password (stored as a salted hash)
- **Profile Information**: filing status, dependents, professional credentials (for Pro+ users)
- **Tax Documents**: W-2s, 1099s, receipts, bank statements, and other financial documents you upload
- **Tax Selections**: deductions, credits, and tax positions you confirm or reject
- **Payment Information**: processed through a PCI-compliant third-party payment processor (Stripe). We do not store full payment card numbers.

### 2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

- **Usage Data**: pages viewed, features used, time spent, interactions with deduction suggestions
- **Device Information**: browser type, operating system, IP address (anonymized after 30 days)
- **Cookies**: essential cookies for authentication and session management only. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Bank Transaction Data**: if you connect a financial account via Plaid or similar service, we receive transaction data you authorize
- **Filing Data**: when you file, your confirmed return data is shared with our third-party e-file provider (FileYourTaxes.com or equivalent) for IRS transmission

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

(a) Provide, maintain, and improve the Service;
(b) Process your document uploads and generate deduction suggestions;
(c) Transmit your confirmed tax return to the IRS through our authorized e-file provider;
(d) Communicate with you about your account, filings, and Service updates;
(e) Comply with legal obligations;
(f) Prevent fraud and abuse.

**We do not:**
- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Use your tax data for advertising or marketing purposes
- Share your data with third parties except as necessary to provide the Service or comply with law

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion

### 4.1 Retention Schedule

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Raw uploaded documents | 30 days after filing, or 12 months if no filing occurs |
| Extracted data | Retained for seasonal reuse (up to 3 years) |
| Audit logs | 3 years (legal requirement) |
| Account information | Until account deletion |
| Usage data (anonymized) | Indefinitely for service improvement |

### 4.2 User-Initiated Deletion

You may request deletion of your data at any time by:
- Using the "Delete My Data" feature within your account settings
- Emailing hello@taxsight.ai

Upon deletion request, we will remove your data within 30 days, subject to legal retention requirements for audit logs.

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

- **Encryption in transit**: all data transmitted via TLS 1.3
- **Encryption at rest**: data stored in encrypted databases using AES-256
- **Access controls**: strict role-based access with audit logging
- **SOC 2 compliance**: we maintain SOC 2 Type II certification (pending) and architect our systems accordingly
- **Regular security assessments**: periodic vulnerability scanning and penetration testing

## 6. Data Sharing

We share your data only in the following circumstances:

### 6.1 Third-Party Service Providers

- **E-file provider**: your confirmed return data is transmitted to FileYourTaxes.com (or equivalent) for IRS e-filing
- **Payment processor**: payment information is processed by Stripe
- **Bank data provider**: if you connect accounts, Plaid (or equivalent) facilitates the connection

Each provider is contractually bound to protect your data and use it only for the specified purpose.

### 6.2 Legal Compliance

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government request, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

### 6.3 Business Transfers

If TaxSight is acquired, merged, or sold, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. You will be notified of any such change.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- **Access**: request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Portability**: request your data in a machine-readable format
- **Objection**: object to certain data processing activities

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@taxsight.ai. We will respond within 30 days.

## 8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect the functionality of the Service.

## 9. Third-Party Links

The Service may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., IRS, Plaid, Stripe). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 11. International Users

The Service is operated in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the U.S., your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy will reflect the most recent changes.
