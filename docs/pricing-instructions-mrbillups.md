# Pricing Research — Instructions for MrBillups

Hey — we're building a TaxSight Pro+ tier for tax professionals (CPAs, EAs, enrolled agents) and need real market pricing data. Here's the assignment:

## What we're building
TaxSight is an AI-first tax tool. For pros, it's:
- Document upload → AI extraction of W-2/1099 data
- Bank transaction import (Plaid) → deduction suggestions
- Professional reviews the AI output, adjusts, confirms
- Filing via third-party API under the pro's EFIN/ERO

We need pricing data on **professional tax software tools only** — ignore consumer products.

## The 4 pricing models we're evaluating

### A. Flat monthly subscription (SaaS)
Base fee per month, includes some returns, additional returns cost extra.

### B. Annual license (like Drake)
One-time per season fee, all returns included.

### C. Pay-per-return (no subscription)
No commitment. Pay only when you file. Buy credits.

### D. Hybrid — subscription + per-return
Low base monthly fee to access the platform, then pay per return filed.

3. **TaxWise** (taxwise.com) — Wolters Kluwer product, popular with seasonal offices. Need: online vs desktop pricing

## What to extract for each product

For each product, get me:
- **Name of each plan/tier** (e.g., Drake Tax Pro vs Drake Tax 1040)
- **Price** per season/annually
- **What's included** (unlimited returns? capped returns? which forms?)
- **User count** (single user, multi-user, unlimited?)
- **Any pay-per-return option** (and price per return)
- **Any monthly subscription option** (not just annual)
- **Free trial available?** (yes/no)
- **e-file pricing** (included? extra fee per return?)
- **State returns** (included in base price? additional fee?)
- **Any "bundles"** — do they offer add-on modules at additional cost?
